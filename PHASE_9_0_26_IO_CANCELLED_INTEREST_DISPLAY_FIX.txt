CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.26 - IO Future Interest Cancelled Display Fix

Fixed Closed IO Closure Summary:
- Future Interest Cancelled was showing ₹0 because cancelled collection rows
  are intentionally removed from the live frontend Collection list after sync.
- CREDNIVO now derives cancelled future interest from:
    Projected post-disbursement IO interest
    minus actual interest payments received.
- Owner extensions are automatically included because Projected Repayment
  increases when extension cycles are added.

Example:
Principal: ₹10,000
Projected Repayment after extension: ₹12,400
Interest paid after disbursement: ₹0
Future Interest Cancelled: ₹2,400

Actual Repayment and Profit Earned calculations are unchanged.
No backend/database change required.

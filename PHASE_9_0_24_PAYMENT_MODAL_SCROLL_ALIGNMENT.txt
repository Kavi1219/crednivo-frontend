CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.24 - Loan Payment Modal Scroll + Alignment

Fixed the Record Loan Payment modal:
- Modal is wider and properly aligned on desktop.
- Header stays fixed at the top.
- Save Payment stays fixed at the bottom.
- Only the middle content scrolls, so the modal always fits inside the screen.
- Payment fields are aligned in a clean two-column layout.
- Payment Date stays full width.
- IO settlement values use four equal summary cards on desktop.
- Mobile uses a bottom-sheet style modal and single-column payment fields.
- Settlement cards become two columns on mobile and one column on very small screens.
- Background page no longer needs to scroll while the payment modal is open.

No backend/database change required.

CREDNIVO Frontend Phase 2.27

- Indian mobile-number handling added across customer/Jamin inputs and displays.
  - Inputs are limited to 10 digits and shown with the +91 prefix.
  - Display format: +91 - 98765 43210.
  - Customer, Jamin, Create Loan selector, Agents and company profile use the same format.
- IO (Interest Only) loan calculation corrected.
  - Collection / Cycle = interest per cycle only.
  - Principal remains outstanding until a principal payment is recorded.
  - If interest is taken upfront, one interest cycle is deducted from amount given and is not counted twice in remaining repayment.
- IO payment flow now separates:
  - Interest Paid
  - Principal Paid
  - Fine Paid
  - Payment Date
- Interest payments do not reduce principal. Principal payments reduce outstanding principal.
- IO-aware payment controls added to Customer Profile, Collections and Overview Today's Collection.
- Existing IO loans receive a one-time frontend accounting migration to principal-based outstanding values.
- Historical payment date behavior remains supported.

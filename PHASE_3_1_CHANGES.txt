CREDNIVO Frontend Phase 3.1 - Collections & Payments Database Flow

- Collections and Payments continue to load from the Spring Boot/PostgreSQL backend.
- Fixed overdue collection payment dates: paying an overdue row no longer silently records the old due date.
- Collection payment popup now includes Payment Date for historical entry and Payment Mode.
- Customer Profile loan payment popup now includes Payment Mode.
- Dashboard quick Pay now records Payment Mode.
- EMI and IO payments are sent through the same backend API payload.
- Payment History now shows payment mode and clearer IO interest/principal breakdown.
- CSV export includes loan, mode, interest, principal and fine values.

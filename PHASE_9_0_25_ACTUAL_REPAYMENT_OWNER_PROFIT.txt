CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.25 - Actual Repayment + Owner-only Customer Profit

Closed loan details:
- Keeps Projected Repayment / Total Repayment for the original plan.
- Adds Actual Repayment after a loan is Closed.
- IO closure summary also shows Principal Repaid and Future Interest Cancelled.
- Fine is shown separately and is never included in Actual Repayment.

Example IO:
Principal 10,000
Upfront interest 200
Two interest payments 200 + 200
Principal settlement 10,000
Actual Repayment = 10,600

Owner-only customer profit:
- New Profit Earned card appears in the top Customer Profile summary only for Owner.
- Agent accounts do not see this profit card.
- Profit = realized interest earned + fine collected.
- IO: uses exact interest payments + upfront interest.
- EMI without upfront interest: realized interest is derived proportionally from actual EMI collections.
- EMI with upfront interest: upfront interest is treated as earned immediately.
- Profit is calculated across every loan belonging to the customer.
- Owner-only Profit Earned is also shown inside a closed loan's Closure Summary.

No backend or database change required.

CREDNIVO PHASE 5.0.3 - CUSTOMER MEDIA FIX

Fixed:
- Customer profile photo is uploaded and persists in Customer Details.
- Customer KYC photo/PDF is uploaded and persists in Customer Details.
- Jamin profile photo and KYC photo/PDF persist.
- Customer/Jamin onboarding media now uses Customer permission, not Documents Vault permission.
- Customer Details receives its linked KYC media from the customer API even when an Agent cannot open the Documents module.
- Frontend validates photo/document sizes before upload and displays a clear error.

CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.12 - Collection Today Summary Fix

Fixed:
- "Collected Today" now means actual collection money received today.
- Early payments made today are included even when their installment due date is tomorrow/later.
- Overdue recoveries paid today are also included.
- "Expected Today" remains based only on installments due today.
- "Pending Today" remains based only on unpaid balance due today.
- "Total Entries" renamed to "Due Today Entries".
- "Paid & Unpaid Entries" renamed to "Today's Due Paid / Unpaid".

Example:
If two future Daily installments of Rs.115 are paid early on 25 Aug:
Expected Today = Rs.0
Collected Today = Rs.230
Pending Today = Rs.0
Due Today Entries = 0
Upcoming advances to the next unpaid installment.

No backend/database change required for this frontend display fix.

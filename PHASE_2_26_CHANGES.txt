CREDNIVO Phase 2.26 - Historical Payment Date

- Added Payment Date to Customer Profile > Record Loan Payment.
- Payment date defaults to today.
- Older dates can be selected for existing customers and historical payment entry.
- Future dates are blocked; dates before the loan disbursed/start date are blocked.
- Payment History and monthly reports now use the selected historical payment date.
- Backdated payments reduce the correct loan/customer outstanding balance without incorrectly increasing Today's Collection.
- If a backdated payment closes a loan, future collection rows for that loan are removed.
- Customer recent payment history is sorted by transaction date.

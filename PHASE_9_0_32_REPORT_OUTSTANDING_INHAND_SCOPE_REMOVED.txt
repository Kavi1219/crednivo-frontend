CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.32 - Reports Business Position + Scope Cleanup

Added to Reports > Overview:
- Total Outstanding
  = current outstanding across active loans.
- In-Hand Amount
  = current Available Capital / available business cash.

These are current business-position snapshots and are intentionally separate
from date-filtered Expected / Collected / Pending / Net Cash Flow metrics.

Monthly Statement:
- Finance Summary now shows In-Hand Amount (Month End).
- Also shows Total Outstanding (Current) as an explicitly labeled current snapshot.

CSV:
- Overview CSV includes Total Outstanding (Current) and In-Hand Amount (Current).
- Monthly CSV includes In-Hand Amount (Month End) and Total Outstanding (Current Snapshot).

UI cleanup:
- Removed the duplicate "Launch Report Scope" section at the bottom.
- Overview / Collection Report / Monthly Statement remain accessible from the top tabs.
- Desktop KPI grid uses 4 columns so the 8 launch KPIs align as two rows.

No backend/database change required.

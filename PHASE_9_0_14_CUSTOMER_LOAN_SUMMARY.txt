CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.14 - Customer Profile Loan Summary

Added to the top Customer Profile card:
- Active Loans: number of currently active loans.
- Pending Due: total unpaid/partial amount whose due date is today or earlier.
- All Loans: total number of loans for the customer.
- Total Outstanding remains visible.
- Current collection amount/cycle remains visible.

Pending Due does NOT count future scheduled installments.
Works on desktop and mobile.
No backend or database change required.

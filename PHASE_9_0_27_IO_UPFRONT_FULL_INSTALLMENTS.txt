CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.27 - IO Upfront Interest Full Installment Rule

Corrected final IO rule:
- Interest Taken Upfront affects only the Given Amount.
- It never consumes or replaces an installment.
- Duration always equals the number of scheduled interest collections.

Example:
Principal: ₹10,000
Interest / cycle: ₹200
Duration: 10 weeks
Interest upfront: Yes

Given Amount: ₹9,800
Scheduled installments: 10 × ₹200
Projected Repayment after disbursement: ₹12,000
Total full-term interest earned including upfront: ₹2,200
Principal Outstanding: ₹10,000

The backend automatically repairs ACTIVE IO loans created under the previous
9-installment rule by adding the missing schedule row and correcting the
Projected Repayment. CLOSED loans are not changed.

No change to EMI rules.

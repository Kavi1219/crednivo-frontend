CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.16 - Partial Payment Status Fix

Validated from Phase 9 payment testing:
- Partial payment is being saved.
- Payment History records the partial collection.
- Loan outstanding reduces correctly.
- The same installment remains in Upcoming with its remaining balance.

Fixed status semantics:
- Future installment + partial payment -> Partial
- Due-today installment + partial payment -> Pending
- Past installment with balance -> Overdue
- Fully paid installment -> Paid
- Future unpaid installment -> Unpaid

Also:
- Added Partial to the Collection status filter.
- Future partial action now says Pay Balance.
- Due-today partial action now says Collect Balance.

Example from test:
Due Rs.115, Paid Rs.65, Balance Rs.50, Due 30 Aug while today is 25 Aug
=> Status: Partial (not Pending)

No backend or database change required.

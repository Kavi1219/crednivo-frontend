CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.22 - Closed Loan Payment Lock

Fixed:
- A Closed loan can no longer open Record Collection from the Collection page.
- Paid historical rows for a Closed loan now show "Closed" instead of "Add Payment".
- Closed action is disabled on desktop and mobile.
- Dashboard Today's Collection also blocks Pay for Closed loans.
- Global frontend recordLoanPayment guard rejects Closed loans or zero-outstanding loans.
- Customer Profile Pay button already remained disabled for Closed loans.

Historical paid rows are still visible for reporting/audit purposes.
No backend or database change required.

CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.29 - IO Partial Principal Repricing Repair

This update makes partial-principal repricing self-healing.

Example:
Principal before payment: ₹10,000
Principal Payment entered: ₹4,000
Remaining Principal: ₹6,000
Interest rate: 2%
New Interest / Cycle: ₹120

Fixes:
- Loans are loaded before collections during frontend sync.
- Backend automatically repairs ACTIVE IO loans whose principal was reduced
  but collection/interest values are still stale.
- Repair uses the latest principal-payment date and changes FUTURE cycles only.
- Customer Loan Details derives current IO interest directly from current principal
  as a display fail-safe.
- "Principal Paid" is renamed "Principal Payment" with clearer help text.
- Preview says "Remaining Principal" to avoid confusing payment amount with balance.

Important:
If a test loan currently shows ₹4,000 outstanding, the database has recorded
₹6,000 of principal payments on that loan. This update will correctly change
its 2% interest to ₹80/cycle; it will not rewrite already-recorded principal
payments. To test the intended ₹4,000 payment case, use a fresh ₹10,000 IO loan
and enter Principal Payment = ₹4,000.

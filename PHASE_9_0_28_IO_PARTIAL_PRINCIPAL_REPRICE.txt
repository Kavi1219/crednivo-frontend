CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.28 - IO Partial Principal Repricing

Final business rule:
- IO interest is always recalculated from the CURRENT principal outstanding.
- Example: Principal ₹10,000 at 2% = ₹200/cycle.
- Customer returns ₹4,000 principal -> principal becomes ₹6,000.
- Next/future interest becomes 2% of ₹6,000 = ₹120/cycle.

Important timing rule:
- Interest already due/pending on or before the principal-payment date is NOT changed.
- Only future collection cycles after the principal-payment date are repriced.
- Fully paid future cycles are never rewritten.
- A partially prepaid future cycle preserves money already paid.

UI:
- Principal payment field shows live New Principal + Next Interest/Cycle preview.
- After saving, Loan Details updates Interest/Cycle and Principal Outstanding.
- Active IO loans with reduced principal show Current Projected Remaining.

Closure reporting:
- New closures persist the exact future interest cancelled so closure summaries
  remain correct even after principal repricing.

No change to EMI rules.

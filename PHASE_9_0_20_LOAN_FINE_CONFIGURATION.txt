CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.20 - Loan Fine Configuration

Loan creation:
- Added Fine applicable? ON/OFF switch.
- Fine OFF: no Fine Amount field is shown.
- Fine ON: Fine Amount field appears immediately.
- Fine Amount must be greater than zero.
- Fine is shown in Review Loan Summary.
- Works for Create Loan and the first loan created during New Customer registration.

Loan details:
- Saved default Fine is shown in Customer -> Loan Details.

Collection behavior:
- Fine stays separate from loan repayment/outstanding.
- The configured Fine does NOT increase Total Repayment.
- When collecting an overdue installment, the configured Fine is automatically
  prefilled in Fine Paid (only when no fine has already been recorded for that entry).
- The collector can still edit the Fine Paid value before saving, subject to permission.

Backend:
- Loan stores fineEnabled and fineAmount.
- No change to interest/EMI/IO calculations.

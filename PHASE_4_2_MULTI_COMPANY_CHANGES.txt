CREDNIVO Phase 4.2 - Multi-Company Workspaces

- Company registration no longer blocks after the first owner.
- Every company creates an isolated workspace.
- Company names may repeat; branch names must be unique.
- Agent registration is matched by exact Company Name + Branch.
- Existing single-company data is migrated to the first existing company by the backend.
- Login remains Owner/Agent; same mobile can belong to multiple companies, but if credentials are ambiguous the user must use username/EMP ID.
- No OTP or email acceptance in this phase.

- Registration screens are compacted for 100% browser zoom on laptop/desktop.
- Registration routes automatically scroll to the top when opened.

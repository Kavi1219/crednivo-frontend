CREDNIVO Phase 9.0.23 - IO Settlement + Owner Extension

IO full settlement:
- Amount to close = Principal Outstanding + ONLY interest already due/pending as of Payment Date.
- No pending interest -> principal only.
- 1 pending cycle -> principal + 1 cycle interest.
- Future IO interest is cancelled when principal is fully returned.
- IO payment modal shows a settlement preview and Use Settlement Amount button.
- IO Total Repayment label is now Projected Repayment.

Owner-only IO extension:
- Active IO loan shows Extend IO only for Owner.
- Owner enters extension cycles + reason.
- Extension adds future interest cycles only.
- Principal does not increase.
- Projected repayment increases only by the added interest.
- Backend independently blocks agents from extension.
